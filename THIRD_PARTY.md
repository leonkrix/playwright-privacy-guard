# Third-party data

## third-party-web

`src/trackers-data.ts` is made from the [third-party-web](https://github.com/patrickhulce/third-party-web) dataset (version in the first line of that file) by `pnpm trackers`.

- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2019 Patrick Hulce (the text is below)
- The data comes from the [HTTP Archive](https://httparchive.org) and from the research of the Lighthouse team.

The package contains a trimmed copy (company, kind of business, domains). The dependency itself is a development dependency and is not installed by users of this package.
